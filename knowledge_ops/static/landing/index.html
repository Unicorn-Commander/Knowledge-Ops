Evidence-first answers

Never take the machine’s word for it.

Ask a question and Knowledge-Ops returns an answer built from cited passages — each citation opens to the exact source, with its scope, owner, and history attached.

  • Every claim carries a chip. Citations link to real passages, not vibes. Open any chip and audit it down to the document, the version, and who can see it.
  • Grounding verdicts are labeled for what they are. Automated grounding is heuristic retrieval — so that’s what we call it. Similarity scores never masquerade as verified fact.
  • Inspect, don’t trust. inspect_citation traces any answer back through the corpus. If the evidence isn’t there, the answer says so.

Governance built in

Memory you can defend in an audit.

A knowledge base is only as trustworthy as what it lets in, who it shows things to, and what it can prove afterwards. Knowledge-Ops treats all three as product, not policy documents.

Reviewed before it’s memory

Nothing enters the corpus unvetted. Every candidate passes through a review queue with provenance, access, privacy, and content checks — approve, edit, or reject, with a paper trail either way.

provenance access privacy content

Scoped to the reader

Global, personal, restricted — scope is enforced at query time, not at write time. The same question returns different evidence for different people, by design. Nobody retrieves what they couldn’t read.

global personal restricted

History & honest deletion

Every document carries version history you can diff. Deletions flow through an audited outbox, so “forgotten” means verifiably removed everywhere — index, store, and citations alike.

doc_history deletion outbox audit log

Agents share the same brain

Your agents ground where your people do.

Knowledge-Ops is MCP-native. Agents call the /mcp endpoint and get the same governed corpus your team curates — same scopes, same review queue, same audit log. No shadow knowledge bases quietly drifting out of date.

  • Storage-opaque. Agents never touch the backing stores. They ask; the service federates, re-ranks, and answers with citations.
  • Governed by default. An agent’s answer is auditable the same way a person’s search is — every tool call lands in the audit log.
  • Honest to machines, too. knowledge.ground returns its verdicts labeled heuristic, so downstream agents can’t launder retrieval into certainty.
